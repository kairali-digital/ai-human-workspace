# Kairali profile

This public profile contains only the rollout identity and approved rescue route. Live
Kairali facts, employee roles, task state and evidence remain in controlled sources.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Company or group label | Kairali |
| Rollout owner decisions | Abhilash |
| Setup/access coordination | Ambuj |
| Shared-system source | This public repository's tagged releases |
| Entity-specific Gate 0 | Not stored in this public profile; create one confirmed private profile per exact legal entity/operating scope |

Use `SETUP-HELPER.md` without shortening it.

Kairali branding is not a compliance profile. A centre operation, healing-village
operation, products business or any other materially different legal entity, operating
unit, jurisdiction or purpose may require different gates. Setup must identify the
exact scope, check current authoritative sources and obtain the named compliance
owner's confirmation. Do not infer certification, tax, medical, legal or other status
from another unit or from an old chart.

The complete approved component bundle is in [`../../packages/kairali`](../../packages/kairali/README.md).
Its homework is the fallback for attendees without a named role plan. Its two governed
skills remain optional and are installed only for a role that needs them.
