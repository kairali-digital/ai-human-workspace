# Product brief

The AI-Human Workspace is a durable local operating system for a person working with
Codex or Claude. The human owns mission, judgment, approvals and final decisions. The
AI may carry out approved work inside exact task, source, tool and Gate 0 boundaries,
while state and proof survive across chats.

v2.4.0 is intended to make that system materially more useful without falsely claiming
unsafe autonomy:

- monthly or quarterly evidence-based personal improvement;
- active, source-linked research across approved official, Reddit and YouTube channels;
- automatic detection of repeated work and friction;
- persistent `PROPOSE / LATER / REJECT` decisions and inactive supervisor-review
  proposals;
- readable, priority-ranked briefs that expose source coverage and decision history;
- owner-supplied before/after time observations, with no inferred money value;
- safer automatic core updates and fleet pilots;
- durable lifecycle recovery and a recoverable pre-v2.4 downgrade export;
- explicit refusal of silent external effects and skill activation until trusted host
  boundaries exist.

The product test is practical: does it reduce rediscovery, surface useful improvements,
preserve control and make errors recoverable for an ordinary client? An impressive
architecture with confusing activation or no measurable time saved is not enough.

The personal-improvement loop is optional and permission-gated. It does not make
external posts, send messages, unsubscribe, change filters, or activate skills. Those
effects remain unavailable until a separate trusted host boundary exists.
