# Adapted frameworks and workflow

## Kairali use

Apply the source concepts as practical questions and checks. Attribute the teaching when material; never present the adapted framework as Kairali's original theory.

## Buyer understanding

- **MoA:** the specific market/avatar being served. Define from current Kairali evidence, not stereotypes.
- **NDFF:** needs, desires, fears and frustrations. A secret desire may inform an MCB only when supported by interviews, customer language, approved research, or other named evidence.
- **ARC:** affinity, shared reality and communication. Use it to enter the buyer's language and context before asking them to enter ours.

## MCB terminology and job

**MCB = Mass Control Bait.** An original Drive PDF uses that expansion and describes an ethical, valuable attractor. A later derived knowledge extraction incorrectly says “Mass Control Bed.” Treat “bed” as a transcription error.

The MCB's job is to create relevant curiosity so the right person raises a hand. It must:

- connect to an evidenced buyer desire or problem;
- remove or reframe a genuine friction without promising the impossible;
- lead to content that fulfils the curiosity;
- avoid clickbait, manipulation, hidden conditions and artificial urgency;
- use only approved claims and proof.

The familiar “how to [desired outcome] without [feared effort/difficulty]” pattern is an option, not a compulsory surface form. The source applies MCB to mass outbound messaging. A web headline that performs the same job is an explicit Kairali adaptation.

## Two Four-Phase models — keep separate

### GO sales/education structure

1. Real Problem
2. Permanent Solution
3. Case Studies
4. Installation

The audited knowledge source places price in Phase 4. Only use a currently authorised price or offer.

### Presentation-content workflow

1. Authority Building
2. Transformational Content
3. Decision Tension
4. CTA / IRO

This comes from a separate prompt set. Do not rename it as the first structure or blend the phase labels.

## Buyer-journey tools

- **KIT:** valuable keep-in-touch content; useful series, not repetitive pressure.
- **IRO / offer:** a low-friction next step with clear conditions and truthful risk treatment. Never create discounts, refunds, guarantees or prices without owner approval.
- **Proof:** approved case studies, qualifications, source-dated evidence and honest limitations. Evidence does not justify a stronger claim than it actually supports.

## Compact create workflow

1. Name the buyer and evidence.
2. Name the buyer-journey stage.
3. Choose one framework job.
4. Draft up to three directions.
5. Verify value fulfilment and claim boundaries.
6. Return a ship gate and one next action.

## Compact review workflow

1. Identify the content's intended job.
2. Check buyer specificity and source evidence.
3. Check that the next content fulfils the hook.
4. Check journey sequence and CTA authority.
5. Remove hype, coercion and unsupported claims.
6. Mark `READY`, `REVISE`, or `BLOCKED`.
