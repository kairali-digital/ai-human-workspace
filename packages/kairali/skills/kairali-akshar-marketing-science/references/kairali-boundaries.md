# Kairali boundaries

## Exact local Gate 0 and public skill risk routing

The active Gate 0 comes only from the current worker's confirmed `GATES.md`. Name the
crossed gate ID and follow its approval/evidence path. This public skill does not define
one Kairali-wide compliance list.

Until the exact local profile and compliance owner explicitly cover them, conservatively
hold the following risk categories for review. This routing list is not itself Gate 0.

Do not authorise or invent:

- medical or treatment claims, dosage, contraindications, clinical outcomes or practitioner advice;
- certification/accreditation wording or scope;
- legal terms, privacy promises, guarantees, refund commitments or regulated wording;
- price, discount, budget, purchase, subscription or spend approval;
- publication, production access, credentials or permission changes.

For these topics, preserve the owner's requested intent, show the precise missing evidence/approver, and return a reviewable draft only.

## Ethical adaptation

- No invented buyer psychology. Use interviews, customer language or labelled research.
- No artificial urgency, fear manipulation, relentless follow-up or clickbait.
- No unsupported superlatives, universal outcomes or copied testimonials.
- No claim that a framework replaces professional sales, medical, legal, product, quality or brand review.
- No repackaging of a trainer's proprietary course as Kairali-owned material.

## Kairali operating rule

“What would Akshar say?” is a marketing review lens, not an author voice and not an automatic approval. Human owners remain accountable for facts, claims, access, spend and release.

## Safe status language

- `READY` — content work is ready for the named human review, not published.
- `REVISE` — evidence-backed improvements are needed.
- `BLOCKED` — a claim, approval, current fact or Gate 0 decision is missing.
