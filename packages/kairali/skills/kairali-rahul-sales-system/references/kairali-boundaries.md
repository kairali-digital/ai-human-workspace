# Kairali boundaries

## Exact local Gate 0 and public skill risk routing

The active Gate 0 comes only from the current worker's confirmed `GATES.md`. Name the
crossed gate ID and follow its approval/evidence path. This public skill does not define
one Kairali-wide compliance list.

Until the exact local profile and compliance owner explicitly cover them, conservatively
hold the following risk categories for review. This routing list is not itself Gate 0.

Do not authorise or invent:

- medical/treatment claims, dosage, contraindications or clinical outcomes;
- certification/accreditation scope;
- legal terms, privacy promises, guarantees or regulated wording;
- prices, discounts, refunds, budgets, subscriptions, targets tied to spend, or purchases;
- hiring, compensation, staffing policy, production access, customer-data access or permissions.

Return the evidence, missing owner/approver and a reviewable proposal only.

## Ethical adaptation

- No gendered, marital-status, age, family-status or other discriminatory hiring assumptions.
- No “client is God” framing that removes professional boundaries or judgment.
- No artificial urgency, fear manipulation, harassment or relentless follow-up.
- No unsupported guarantee, target, staffing ratio, conversion prediction or performance uplift.
- No blind transfer of manufacturing/distribution methods into healthcare, wellness, hospitality, retail or digital work.
- No repackaging of a trainer's proprietary course as Kairali-owned material.

## Change boundary

A process proposal is not permission to reorganise staff, change a CRM, expose customer data, alter access, purchase a tool, contact customers, publish an offer or change policy. Keep those actions behind named owner decisions.

## Safe status language

- `READY TO PILOT` — the bounded proposal is ready for owner approval, not implemented.
- `REVISE` — evidence or design needs improvement.
- `BLOCKED` — a current fact, high-risk approval or Gate 0 decision is missing.
