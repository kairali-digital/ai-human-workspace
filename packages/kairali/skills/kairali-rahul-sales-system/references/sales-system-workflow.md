# Adapted sales-system workflow

## Operating idea

Sales should be observable and improvable rather than dependent on a single person's memory or heroics. Adapt the source principles to Kairali's actual business, approved tools and customer obligations.

## Process map

Map the current work before redesigning it:

1. lead source and qualification;
2. contact and discovery;
3. value/proof and proposal;
4. objection/decision support;
5. order and handoff;
6. fulfilment/service/collection where relevant;
7. retention, reorder and referral where appropriate.

For every stage record:

- owner;
- trigger/input;
- customer-facing action;
- operational support;
- system/evidence;
- handoff and done condition;
- exception/escalation.

## NBD and CRR

- **NBD:** new business development and genuinely new opportunity creation.
- **CRR:** current-customer retention/reorder work and the customer-service operations that support it.

Do not assume the source's manufacturing/distribution split fits healthcare, hospitality, retail or digital work. Name the local buyer journey and adapt deliberately.

## Division of labour

Ask which tasks require selling judgment and which are repeatable coordination, data, scheduling, proposal, order, service or follow-up operations. Move work only through an owner-approved pilot with clear customer continuity. Do not use this principle to justify layoffs, discriminatory staffing assumptions or arbitrary ratios.

## SPIN discovery spine

Keep SPIN as Kairali's conversation method:

- Situation — understand the relevant context without interrogating.
- Problem — surface the customer's real difficulty.
- Implication — explore the consequence honestly, without fear manipulation.
- Need-payoff — help the customer articulate useful value and the right next step.

Record the customer's words, approved next action and owner. Do not diagnose clinically or promise outcomes.

## POST support model

Use POST as a preparation/review lens where helpful:

- Pitch/value communication;
- Objection handling;
- Sales tools/proof;
- Training/practice;
- plus an explicit follow-up and handoff discipline.

## Measurement and review

Prefer stage evidence and actions to unsupported forecasts. Possible fields—only when already authorised—include:

- opportunities entering/exiting each stage;
- time to next action;
- agreed follow-ups completed;
- handoff completeness;
- documented reason for loss/stall;
- call observation/roleplay completion;
- manager sign-off and retraining action.

Never invent targets or benchmark ratios. Use the owner's current authorised figures or mark `UNKNOWN`.

## Pilot pattern

1. Select one role, product, channel or location.
2. Capture the current baseline from actual evidence.
3. Define one process change and its owner.
4. Preserve a rollback/recovery route.
5. Observe customer continuity, workload, data quality and stage movement.
6. Review with the people doing the work.
7. Owner decides whether to revise, stop or scale.
