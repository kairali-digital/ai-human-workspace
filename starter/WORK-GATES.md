# WORK GATES

Task-specific operating locks belong here. They may narrow the worker's actions but
must never replace, weaken or impersonate the confirmed entity-specific Gate 0 in
`GATES.md`.

## Current task-specific locks

- The action must belong to the live task and `ROLE.md` scope.
- Available tools do not grant permission; `TOOLBOX.md` controls tool use.
- External or difficult-to-reverse effects require the recorded approval path.
  v2.4 has no active standing-permission executor. Future support would require the
  trusted external authority described in `.ai-human/system/AUTONOMY-CONTROL.md`;
  it could never replace Gate 0.
- The live task's exit evidence, ledger and evidence log control completion.

Add a role-specific lock only from an approved source. Record owner rulings in
`DECISIONS.md`; do not turn a remembered practice into a company compliance gate.
